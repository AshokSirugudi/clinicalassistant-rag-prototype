# Keeping a Home Blood Pressure Log

Fictional general guidance for the Clinical RAG prototype.

## What to Record

Record the date, time, and both numbers displayed for each reading. Note relevant context, such as whether the reading was taken after activity.

## Organizing the Log

Use one row per reading and keep entries in date order. Mark gaps so they are easy to distinguish from recorded readings.

## Preparing for Follow-Up

Bring the log and note any patterns or questions about readings that varied.
