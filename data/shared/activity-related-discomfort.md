# Tracking Discomfort During Activity

Fictional general guidance for the Clinical RAG prototype.

## Describing Discomfort

Record where the discomfort occurred, what it felt like, and when it began during the activity.

## Tracking Activity and Function

Note the activity, how long it continued, and whether discomfort changed what could be done. Include examples from everyday tasks such as walking or typing.

## Preparing for Follow-Up

Summarize which activities were most affected and whether the pattern changed over time.
