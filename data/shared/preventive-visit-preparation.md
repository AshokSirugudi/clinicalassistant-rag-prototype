# Preparing for a Routine Preventive Visit

Fictional general guidance for the Clinical RAG prototype.

## Before the Visit

Gather available health history, a current medication and supplement list, and any relevant screening or vaccination records.

## Questions to Bring

Write down the preventive care topics and routine changes worth discussing. Put the most important questions first.

## Recording Follow-Up Items

Keep a brief list of follow-up items discussed during the visit. Record who will handle each item and any agreed timing.
