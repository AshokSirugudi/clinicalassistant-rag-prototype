# Keeping a Headache Diary

Fictional general guidance for the Clinical RAG prototype.

## What to Record

For each headache, note when it started, how long it lasted, how intense it felt, and which activities it interrupted.

## Noting Possible Triggers

Record context such as changes in sleep, meal timing, or routines. Describe what happened before a headache without assuming it caused the episode.

## Preparing for a Headache Review

Bring the diary and highlight recurring patterns or changes in frequency, duration, or impact.
